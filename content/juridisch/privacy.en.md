## What data do I process?

**Through the contact form:** your name, your email address, the name of your company or organisation if you fill it in, and your message.

**By email or phone:** the details you share yourself, such as your name, contact details and the content of your question.

**Technical data:** like any web server, the server of this website keeps a log that includes your IP address, the time and the page requested. This is used only to keep the website secure and working properly.

This website uses **no cookies**, no analytics services and no trackers. Fonts and images are loaded directly from this server, not through external services.

## Why, and on what legal basis?

- **To answer your question and prepare a quote if needed.** Basis: steps needed to enter into a contract at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), or my legitimate interest in replying to messages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **To secure the website and detect problems** using server logs. Basis: my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **To meet legal obligations**, such as the duty to keep accounting records if you become a client (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

I do not use your data for marketing and I never sell it.

## Who receives your data?

I work with a few carefully chosen providers that process data only on my behalf:

- **OVHcloud** (France): hosting of this website.
- **Brevo** (France): sending the email that the contact form sends to me.
- **Microsoft 365**: my business mailbox, where your message arrives.

If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, this is done with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long do I keep your data?

- **Messages and quote requests** that do not lead to an assignment: no longer than 2 years after our last contact.
- **Client data:** for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as the law requires (10 years for invoices and accounting).
- **Server logs:** no longer than 14 days.

## How is your data protected?

The website is only reachable over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The server is kept up to date and only I have access to it.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing and to data portability. To exercise these rights, email [aiko@mobiussystems.be](mailto:aiko@mobiussystems.be). I reply within one month.

If you are not satisfied with how your data is handled, you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, [www.dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be).

I may update this policy, for example when the website changes. The date of the latest update is shown at the top.
